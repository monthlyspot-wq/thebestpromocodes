Gold Coins vs Sweeps Coins: How Sweepstakes Casinos Work

Operator documents checked: 2026-09-28 · Page updated: 2026-09-28

General explainer, not legal advice. Rules vary by operator and by state, and operators change them. The operator's current documents always decide.

Short answer

A sweepstakes casino runs two currencies. One is a play-only coin (Gold Coins, or WOW Coins at Wow Vegas) that can't be redeemed for prizes. The other is a sweepstakes coin (Stake Cash, Shuffle Cash, Sweep Coins, Sweeps Cash or SC). The operators give it away free rather than selling it, and once it's won in play and their conditions are met, it can be redeemed for prizes.

Each operator also publishes a free way to get sweepstakes coins, including a mail-in request. These platforms aren't NJ-licensed online casinos, and every sweepstakes operator on this site restricts New Jersey.

“No purchase necessary” and mail-in entry (AMOE)

The operators say you don't need to buy anything to take part. Stake.us's terms say “NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT IS NECESSARY TO PARTICIPATE OR PLAY THE GAMES”, and MyPrize's Official Rules say “NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN.” Both add that a purchase won't increase your chances of winning. Some operators add free sweepstakes coins to specific Gold Coin packs.

The free route the rules spell out is a written request by post, often called an AMOE (alternative method of entry). The rules are strict about format. Shuffle.us's, RealPrize's and MyPrize's rules all require handwritten details that match your account, one request per envelope, and mailing from the same state as the verified address on your account. The rules also list other free sources, such as daily bonuses and giveaways.

FAQ

What is the difference between Gold Coins and Sweeps Coins?

Gold Coins (WOW Coins at Wow Vegas) are for play only, and the operators say they cannot be redeemed for prizes. The sweepstakes currency (Stake Cash, Shuffle Cash, Sweep Coins, Sweeps Cash or SC) is given out free and can be redeemed for prizes under each operator's rules.

What does AMOE or mail-in entry mean?

AMOE stands for alternative method of entry: the free route to sweepstakes coins described in each operator's rules, usually a handwritten request sent by post. The amount per request differs by operator.

Can you buy Sweeps Coins?

The operators say no. Stake.us, Shuffle.us and RealPrize say their sweepstakes currency cannot be purchased, and Wow Vegas says SC cannot be purchased directly. Some add free sweepstakes coins to Gold Coin packs.

Are sweepstakes casinos available in New Jersey?

Not the ones on this site. Stake.us, Shuffle.us, RealPrize and MyPrize list New Jersey as restricted or unavailable, and Wow Vegas lists New Jersey under locations restricted from SC play. NJ-licensed online casinos are a separate section of this site.
