Free spins / bonus spins

Spins on designated slots (or other games) credited as a promo. Winnings, wagering, and expiry are set by the operator.

DraftKings Casino spin product used in some welcome offers (e.g. Play $5 → 1,000 Flex Spins for new casino players). Exact Flex Spins rules are on DraftKings.

Playthrough / wagering

Welcome offer
